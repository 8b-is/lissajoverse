lineage

Oscilloscope music — Jerobeam Fenderson, Spirals (Oscilloscope Music), where a stereo signal is the drawing: L→X, R→Y, one channel per axis.
The hardware — the Ultra-Simple Oscilloscope: two builds of one idea, a solid-state driver (~200 V at ~220 kHz) and a tube build (~300 V), a push-pull stage that drives positive and pulls negative, the high voltage struck by a flyback transformer with a fast-recovery diode. The eye is a 2AP1 — 1.25″ round, electrostatic deflection, P1 green, or the dual-layer P7 that flashes blue-white and lingers yellow-green. The Tektronix 5103N is where the album was filmed from.
The shelf — the diatribe papers; the wiki, crawled with robots.txt respect.
The instruments above — AMNH Hayden Planetarium · Digital Universe Atlas · Uniview (SCISS).
